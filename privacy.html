🔐 Privacy Policy

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24
Privacy & Data Protection

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ALON HISTORYVERSE 24, created and managed by Baba Thecno Guru.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, why that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

हमारी प्राथमिकता / Our Principle:
We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for providing website features and improving the user experience.

2. Information That May Be Used

Depending on the features you use, the website may process the following information:

Information Possible Purpose
Email address Login, account identification and account-related features.
Mobile number User identification and account-related features.
Country / Calling Code International user identification and country-specific features.
User ID Identifying a user's activity without displaying sensitive account credentials.
Location Features that specifically require live location, only after browser permission is granted.
Books / Articles / Comments Providing library, reading, article and community features.

3. Password Information

Passwords are sensitive account credentials. The frontend login system is designed not to display or expose a user's password to other website visitors.

Important: The current GitHub Pages implementation is a client-side website system. It is not a replacement for a secure server-side authentication service.

A future server-side authentication system may be introduced if secure multi-user account management is required.

4. Live Location

Some features may request access to your device's location.

  • Location access is controlled by your browser.
  • The browser normally asks for permission before providing location information.
  • You can deny location permission.
  • Location-dependent features may not work when permission is denied.
  • The website should not request location when the feature does not require it.

5. Browser Storage

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 may use browser storage such as localStorage and sessionStorage for website functionality.

Examples may include:

  • Login session information
  • Anonymous User ID
  • Reading history
  • Book preferences
  • Like / Dislike information
  • Comments and reactions
  • Saved website settings

Clearing your browser's website data may remove information stored locally on your device.

6. Books, Articles and User Contributions

Future and existing website features may allow users to interact with books, articles and other educational content.

  • Users may be able to read books.
  • Users may like or dislike content.
  • Users may submit comments or reactions.
  • A generated User ID may be associated with reading or contribution activity.
  • Uploaded content may be subject to the website's contribution and copyright rules.

7. Sharing of Information

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 does not intend to publicly display sensitive login credentials such as passwords.

Information may be processed or shared when reasonably necessary for:

  • Operating a requested website feature.
  • Preventing misuse or abuse.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.
  • Using a future third-party service that the user has explicitly chosen to use.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may eventually use third-party services for features such as hosting, authentication, analytics, maps, storage, content delivery or other functionality.

When such services are introduced, their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

9. Children and Young Users

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 is intended primarily as an educational and historical knowledge platform.

Users should not submit unnecessary personal information, especially information belonging to children or another person.

10. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect website information and user data.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through ordinary comments or public content fields.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the available website features, users may be able to:

  • Log out of their account.
  • Remove locally stored browser data.
  • Deny browser location permission.
  • Remove or manage their own submitted content where the website provides that functionality.
  • Contact the website owner regarding privacy concerns.

12. Data Retention

Data may be retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the relevant website feature, security, legal or operational purpose.

Locally stored data can generally be removed by clearing the website's stored browser data.

13. Copyright and Content

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 is intended to provide historical, cultural, educational and knowledge resources.

Users should not upload or submit copyrighted material unless they have the necessary rights, permission or a lawful basis to do so.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, technology, data practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version published on this page should be considered the current version.

15. Privacy Questions

If you have a privacy-related question, concern, correction request or data-related issue, please use the contact facility provided by ALON HISTORYVERSE 24.
