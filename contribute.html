✅ Good Contribution Checklist

  • Give the information a clear title.
  • Explain the subject in enough detail.
  • Mention dates and locations where known.
  • Provide reliable sources or references.
  • Separate established facts from uncertain claims.
  • Do not submit copied copyrighted material without the necessary permission.
  • Do not submit private or confidential information.
What happens after submission?
A future connected contribution system can send submitted material to an administrative review area. Content can then be checked, edited, approved or rejected before publication.
Why are sources important?
Sources allow historical and educational information to be checked and help improve the reliability of the knowledge collection.
Can images and videos be included?
Yes, media information can be provided, but contributors should make sure they have the appropriate rights or permissions to submit the material.

🔎 Contribution Review Principles

  • Accuracy and clarity should be prioritized.
  • Historical claims should be supported by appropriate references whenever possible.
  • Content may require editing before publication.
  • Duplicate, misleading, unsafe or unsupported submissions may not be published.
  • Copyright and licensing should be respected.
