📋 Contact Guidelines

  • Be clear and specific when describing your question.
  • For a content correction, mention the page, section and information that needs review.
  • For a technical problem, describe the device, browser and exact problem when possible.
  • Do not submit passwords, verification codes, financial information or other sensitive data.
  • For copyright-related matters, provide enough information to identify the relevant content.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report an incorrect fact?
Select “Content Correction” as the subject and clearly identify the page and information that needs review.
Can I contribute historical information?
Yes. Use the contribution subject and describe the material or knowledge you would like to submit.
What should I include in a technical report?
Include the affected page, what you expected to happen, what actually happened and, when useful, your browser and device type.
Does this form automatically send an email?
No. This standalone page validates the form locally. Actual email delivery needs a connected backend or trusted form service.
Can I use this page on a mobile phone?
Yes. The contact layout is designed to adapt to smaller screens and touch devices.
