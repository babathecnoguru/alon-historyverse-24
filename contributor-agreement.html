Contributor Agreement

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24

A contribution and publishing agreement for historical, educational, cultural and knowledge-based content submitted to ALON HISTORYVERSE 24.

Creator / Owner: Baba Thecno Guru

1. Introduction

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 is a knowledge platform focused on history, civilizations, countries, culture, heritage, timelines, education and related subjects.

This Contributor Agreement explains the conditions under which a contributor may submit material for consideration, publication, editing, correction or removal on the platform.

Important: Submitting material does not automatically guarantee publication. Every contribution may be reviewed before appearing publicly.

2. Contributor Eligibility

A contributor submitting content should provide accurate information and should have the necessary rights or permissions to submit the material.

  • Provide truthful contributor information.
  • Submit content in good faith.
  • Respect copyright and intellectual property rights.
  • Do not knowingly submit fabricated historical claims.
  • Do not submit unlawful or harmful material.

3. Originality and Accuracy

Contributors are responsible for the material they submit. Historical information should be prepared carefully and, whenever reasonably possible, supported by reliable sources.

Contributors should:

  • Check names, dates and locations before submission.
  • Distinguish historical facts from interpretation.
  • Provide references for important claims.
  • Correct known errors when they are discovered.
  • Avoid deliberately misleading information.

4. Sources and References

Contributors are encouraged to provide books, academic publications, archives, museums, official records, educational resources and other reliable references.

Sources may be used by the review process to verify information before publication.

Source rule: Do not present another person's work as your own. When information comes from another source, identify the source appropriately.

5. Copyright and Intellectual Property

Contributors must have the right to submit any text, photograph, illustration, graphic, video or other material included in their contribution.

  • Do not upload copyrighted material without permission.
  • Do not copy complete articles from another website.
  • Do not submit pirated books, scans or protected files.
  • Provide attribution where attribution is required.
  • Clearly identify material that requires a license or permission.

6. Images, Photographs and Graphics

Images submitted with a contribution must be legally usable by the contributor or properly licensed for the intended use.

  • Provide the original creator when known.
  • Provide the source or license information when applicable.
  • Do not upload private photographs without permission.
  • Do not remove copyright notices from protected material.

7. Videos and External Media

Contributors may provide information about relevant videos or external media. The contributor should provide the source and appropriate ownership or licensing information.

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 may choose whether an external media item is appropriate for the platform.

8. Contributor Rights

Recognition

Where appropriate, contributors may be credited for accepted material.

Corrections

Contributors may report factual errors or request corrections to information they submitted.

Questions

Contributors may contact the project regarding their submitted material.

Review

Submitted material may be reviewed before publication.

9. Platform Review and Editorial Rights

ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 may review, edit, format, categorize, organize or decline submitted material when necessary for accuracy, quality, legal compliance or platform standards.

Minor editorial changes may include spelling, grammar, formatting, headings, summaries and structural organization.

Material may also be removed if it is later found to be inaccurate, infringing, inappropriate or inconsistent with platform guidelines.

10. Prohibited Contributions

Contributors must not knowingly submit:

  • Plagiarized content.
  • False historical information presented as fact.
  • Copyright-infringing material.
  • Malware, malicious files or harmful code.
  • Fraudulent documents or fabricated sources.
  • Spam or unrelated promotional material.
  • Content intended to deceive users.
  • Material that violates applicable law.

11. Moderation and Removal

Submitted content may be reviewed by the platform's administrators or editorial process.

A contribution may be rejected, edited, unpublished, archived or removed when there is a reasonable need to protect accuracy, copyright, users or the integrity of ALON HISTORYVERSE 24.

12. Contributor Information

Information supplied through a contribution form should be limited to information necessary for contribution, communication, verification and administration.

Contributors should not submit passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information through a contribution form.

13. Contributor Responsibility

The contributor remains responsible for ensuring that the submitted material can lawfully be provided for review and potential publication.

Contributors should promptly report any copyright issue, factual error or ownership problem concerning their submitted material.

14. Agreement Acceptance

By checking the acceptance box below, the contributor confirms that they have read and understood this agreement and intend to follow these contribution principles.

Note: This web form records your acknowledgement locally in this browser. A production submission and permanent contributor record require a secure backend/database.

15. Final Understanding

The purpose of this agreement is to maintain a reliable, respectful and useful knowledge environment for ALON HISTORYVERSE 24 contributors and readers.

By contributing, users are encouraged to help preserve historical knowledge while respecting accuracy, sources, copyright and the work of other contributors.
